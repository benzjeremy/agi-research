## 2. Why Mainstream Developers Failed to Build AGI

Mainstream AI laboratories (OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic) have delayed true AGI due to three structural blind spots:

1. **The Scaling Law Trap**: Attempting to solve reasoning by inflating parameter counts (100B -> 1T -> 10T) rather than improving agentic architecture.
2. **The Frozen Weight Delusion**: Shipping models with static weights. An intelligence that cannot update its long-term episodic memory during operation is merely a static archive.
3. **The Chatbot Bias**: Designing AI to passively wait for user input, ignoring intrinsic curiosity drives and self-directed goal formulation.
